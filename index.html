Lyra

Generate short-form lyric edits and post them on your socials.

Lyra (project codename for ContentFarm) is a personal, locally-run desktop application that turns songs into audio-reactive short-form video reels with automatically generated synced lyrics (auto-lyrics) and karaoke-style captions.

Main features:

  • Automatic speech-to-text transcription and lyric syncing from an audio track
  • Audio-reactive animations and customizable subtitle styles
  • Local rendering of the final video, ready to publish
  • Optional TikTok account connection to streamline publishing your renders

Lyra runs entirely on the user's own machine. It is not a hosted multi-user web service: each user runs their own local instance and connects their own TikTok account.
