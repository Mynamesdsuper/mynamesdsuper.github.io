Lyra

Turn a song into a lyric video, synced automatically, ready to publish.

Overview

Lyra is a locally-run application that turns any song into an audio-reactive short-form video reel with automatically generated, precisely synced lyrics (auto-lyrics) and karaoke-style captions. Drop in an audio track and a folder of video clips, and Lyra transcribes the vocals, times each word to the beat, and composes a finished, ready-to-share vertical video.

Everything runs on the user's own machine. Lyra is not a hosted, multi-tenant web service — there is no central server processing your media, and each user works with their own local files and, optionally, their own connected TikTok account.

Features

  • Automatic lyric transcriptionSpeech-to-text transcription and word-level timing extracted directly from the audio track, no manual subtitle editing required.
  • Beat-synced clip editingVideo clips are cut and sequenced automatically in time with the music's beat.
  • Karaoke-style captionsCustomizable subtitle styles, colors, animations, and word-by-word highlight, with an option to render clips only, without burned-in text.
  • Local renderingThe final video is rendered entirely on the user's device and saved locally, ready to publish.
  • Stock footage searchSearch and import clips directly into the local clip library to use as render material.
  • Optional TikTok connectionSecurely connect a TikTok account via TikTok's official Login Kit to streamline publishing finished reels, including scheduled bulk posting.

How it works

  1. Import an audio track and a library of video clips.
  2. Lyra transcribes the lyrics and times each word automatically.
  3. Customize the caption style, or turn captions off for a clips-only render.
  4. Render the final vertical video locally.
  5. Optionally connect a TikTok account and publish, or schedule a batch of reels for later.
