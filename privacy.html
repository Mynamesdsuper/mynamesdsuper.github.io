Privacy Policy

Lyra (ContentFarm) — last updated 2026-08-22

Lyra is a personal, locally-run application. It does not operate a backend server, does not host user accounts, and does not collect, sell, or share personal data with any third party.

1. Audio and video files

Audio and video files you process with Lyra are read from and written to your own device. They are never uploaded to any remote server operated by Lyra.

2. TikTok account connection

If you choose to connect a TikTok account, Lyra requests the minimum scope needed (currently user.info.basic) to display your public profile name and avatar inside the application. This is done through TikTok's official OAuth flow; Lyra never sees or stores your TikTok password.

  • Your TikTok access and refresh tokens are stored only in a local file on your own device, never transmitted anywhere except directly to TikTok's own API endpoints.
  • No TikTok data is sent to any server other than TikTok's own API.
  • You can disconnect at any time from within the application; this revokes the token and deletes it from local storage immediately.

3. Analytics and tracking

Lyra does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.

4. Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app developer through the TikTok Developer Portal contact associated with this application.
